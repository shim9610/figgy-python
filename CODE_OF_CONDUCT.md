# Code of conduct

We welcome contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

- Discuss the code and evidence respectfully; criticize proposals without
  attacking the person.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats and unwanted personal contact.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's private information.
- Keep issues and reviews useful: provide reproducible examples, acknowledge
  uncertainty and leave room for people to correct mistakes.

These expectations apply to this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions
and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content,
close disruptive threads, warn participants, or restrict participation. Responses
depend on the conduct and its impact; repeated or serious abuse may result in
a ban.

Report concerns privately to **ssy9610@gmail.com**. Include relevant links and
context while avoiding unnecessary personal data. Reports are handled as
confidentially as practical; information may be shared when needed to investigate
or address the report. Maintainers are also bound by this policy.
